# Document settings

Paper size, print quality, page count, page numbers, and multi-page mode toggle.

## Paper Size

| Value | Dimensions | Dominant region |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| `a4` | 210×297 mm | Rest of world (schools, UK, EU, Asia, AU) |
| `letter` | 216×279 mm (8.5×11 in) | United States and Canada |
| `legal` | 216×356 mm (8.5×14 in) | US contracts, legal pads |
| `a5` | 148×210 mm | Half-sheets, take-home practice |

**Cases:**
- **Classroom set**: Letter (US) or A4 (everywhere else). 300 DPI. 25 pages.
- **Take-home pack**: A5 with 5 pages. 150 DPI for small file size.
- **Wall poster**: Letter or A4, single page, 600 DPI for maximum print quality.
- **Legal document practice**: Legal paper, narrow spacing, 150 DPI.

**Recommendation**: A4 with High (300 DPI) print quality for most cases. Switch to Letter only when printing in US/Canada.

---

## Print Quality

| Value | DPI | Scale | File size | Use case |
|-------|-----|-------|-----------|----------|
| `standard` | 150 DPI | 1.56× | Small | Screen-only, quick drafts, student digital work |
| `high` | 300 DPI | 3.13× | Medium | Most classroom printing (default) |
| `ultra` | 600 DPI | 6.25× | Large | Professional publishing, final student portfolios |

**Case:**
- A teacher printing 30 worksheets for a class: High (300 DPI). At 150 DPI, text may appear slightly pixelated.
- A parent printing one worksheet at home: Ultra (600 DPI) produces crispest output even on home printers.
- Exporting for Google Classroom / digital distribution: Standard (150 DPI). Smaller file, faster upload.

**Recommendation**: High (300 DPI) covers all classroom needs. Ultra only when print quality matters more than file size.

---

## Page Count

- Range: 1 to 10 pages.
- Only available in single-page mode (`multiPageMode: false`).
- Each page is identical. For different content per page, enable multi-page mode.

**Cases:**
- 1 page: Quick warm-up exercise.
- 5 pages: Weekly handwriting packet.
- 10 pages: Full lesson worksheet packet for a week.

---

## Show Page Numbers

- Adds "Page 1 of 5" at the bottom-right of each PDF page.
- Shows only when both `showPageNumbers` is true AND `pageNumber`/`totalPages` are provided (in PDF export and preview).

**Cases:**
- ON: Printed workbook where students need to keep pages in order.
- OFF: Single-page handout or where page count is distracting.

---

## Footer Text

- Fixed value: `"Generated by InkTrace"`
- Not user-editable. Shows at the top of each page (branding/URL).

**Why fixed**: Free tool attribution. Keeps the tool discoverable when worksheets are shared.

---

## Multi-Page Mode

When enabled:
- Page Count slider disappears (replaced by page list).
- Pages section becomes active for page-level editing.
- Content, Text sections show "managed in Pages" or work globally.

**Cases:**
- OFF: Standard single-type worksheet — all pages identical.
- ON: Progression worksheet — pages 1-2 trace letters, page 3 copy words, page 4 free writing.
